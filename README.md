The world is already under pressure
The network is lighting up. Accounts are behaving strangely. Endpoints are raising alarms. Logs disagree, the scope is unclear, and the clock is not waiting for certainty.
Welcome to IRONCLAD — a cyber-defense training simulation built around the moments that matter after a suspicious signal appears. You step into the role of an incident responder, working through a growing series of security scenarios where the goal is not simply to react quickly, but to understand what is happening and choose a response that makes sense.
An alert is not a verdict. A suspicious indicator is not the whole story. And the biggest response is not always the best one.

Your job is to connect the evidence, assess the risk, and make the call.
Every incident is a decision under uncertainty
IRONCLAD turns incident response into a mission-driven experience. Each scenario presents a security problem, a set of evidence to examine, and a decision that shapes the outcome.
- Investigate the signal. Explore incident evidence such as event timelines, identity and asset context, audit telemetry, and potential scope or impact.
- Connect the clues. Look for relationships between activity, entities, and indicators instead of treating every alert as an isolated event.
- Choose your response. Decide whether the situation calls for targeted containment, further monitoring, preservation, mitigation, recovery, or escalation.
- Face the debrief. See how your choice is assessed and why proportionality, evidence preservation, and validation matter.
- Build your record. Earn experience, complete levels, and continue progressing through the campaign.
The challenge is not to click the most dramatic option. It is to make a defensible decision with the information available.

Your decisions have consequences
A security incident rarely offers a perfect, risk-free answer. Moving too slowly can leave a threat active. Acting too broadly can interrupt legitimate work, destroy useful context, or create a second incident while trying to stop the first.
IRONCLAD's response debriefs reinforce the judgement behind effective defense:
- Proportionate action — respond to the evidence without creating unnecessary disruption.
- Evidence first — preserve the information needed to understand what happened.
- Scope before shutdown — determine what is affected before expanding the blast radius.
- Verify the result — mitigation is not complete until its effect has been checked.
- Keep investigating — containment is a milestone, not the end of the story.
The game rewards incident-response thinking, not just urgency.

Built to feel like a command center
IRONCLAD pairs its incident-response scenarios with a dark, high-contrast cyber operations interface: animated backgrounds, glowing cyan accents, radar-inspired visuals, mission briefings, evidence panels, and post-incident debriefs.
The atmosphere is deliberate. You are not just reading about a security incident — you are stepping into a simulation designed to make investigation and response feel like a connected operation.
A learning simulation, not a live security tool
IRONCLAD is designed for learning and simulated incident-response practice. Its scenarios and evidence are presented within the game; it is not a live SOC platform, endpoint agent, threat-intelligence feed, or incident-management system. It does not need access to a real organisation's network to play through its scenarios.
Use it to explore the reasoning behind security decisions, practise structured investigation, and reflect on the trade-offs between speed, scope, and operational impact.
